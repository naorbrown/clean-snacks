## Steps

1. Add the banana (broken into chunks), blueberries, and yogurt to a blender. Using frozen fruit produces a thicker, colder smoothie. If all ingredients are fresh, add 3-4 ice cubes for a chilled result.

2. Add the raw honey and melted coconut oil. The coconut oil should be in liquid form — if it solidifies on contact with cold ingredients, it will create small waxy chunks. Melting it first and adding it while the blender is running prevents this.

3. Blend on high speed for 30-45 seconds until completely smooth. There should be no visible chunks of fruit, seed fragments, or yogurt streaks. A smooth texture is especially important for easy swallowing.

4. Check the consistency. If the smoothie is too thick to sip through a straw or drink comfortably from a glass, add water or milk one tablespoon at a time and blend briefly until you reach a pourable consistency.

5. Pour into a glass and serve immediately. Use a wide straw if preferred — the slight thickness of the smoothie makes it easier to control the flow compared to thin liquids, which can be helpful for anyone with swallowing difficulties.

## Why It Works

Blueberries are among the highest antioxidant foods available, rich in anthocyanins that support brain health and cognitive function. The full-fat yogurt provides calcium and protein along with probiotics that support digestive health — a combination that becomes increasingly important with age. Blending transforms whole fruits into a drinkable format that delivers all the fiber and nutrients of the original ingredients without requiring any chewing, and the coconut oil adds easily digestible medium-chain triglycerides for quick energy.

## Tips

- **Protein boost.** Add a scoop of collagen powder for an extra 10 grams of protein that dissolves completely and does not change the flavor or texture of the smoothie.
- **Thickness.** Frozen banana makes the smoothie thick and creamy like a milkshake. Fresh banana produces a thinner, more juice-like consistency. Use whichever texture is more comfortable to drink.
- **Meal replacement.** For a more substantial meal, add 2 tablespoons of almond butter and an extra half banana. This increases the calorie and protein content enough to serve as a light breakfast or lunch.
