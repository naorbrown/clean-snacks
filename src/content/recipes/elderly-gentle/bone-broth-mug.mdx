## Steps

1. Pour the bone broth into a small saucepan and place over medium heat. If using homemade broth that has gelled in the fridge, it will liquefy as it warms. Stir occasionally to ensure even heating.

2. Add the turmeric and black pepper once the broth begins to steam. Whisk gently to dissolve the turmeric completely — undissolved turmeric will settle at the bottom and create a gritty last sip.

3. Heat until the broth is steaming hot but not at a rolling boil. A gentle simmer preserves the delicate collagen proteins and keeps the broth's flavor clean and mellow.

4. Pour into a large, comfortable mug. Add sea salt and stir. Taste and adjust — the right amount of salt will bring all the savory depth of the broth forward without tasting salty.

5. Let the mug sit for 2-3 minutes until it is comfortable to sip. Drink slowly throughout the morning or afternoon as a warming, hydrating snack.

## Why It Works

Bone broth made from long-simmered bones is rich in collagen, glycine, and the minerals calcium, magnesium, and phosphorus that leach from the bones during cooking. Collagen supports joint health, gut lining integrity, and skin elasticity — all areas of particular importance as the body ages. The liquid format makes it exceptionally easy to consume for anyone with reduced appetite or difficulty eating solid foods, and the warmth itself provides comfort and promotes relaxation.

## Tips

- **Broth quality.** A good bone broth gels when cold — that gel is collagen. If your store-bought broth does not gel, it was likely not simmered long enough to extract the collagen. Look for brands that list bones as the first ingredient and contain visible gelatin.
- **Timing.** Drink a mug of bone broth mid-morning or mid-afternoon as a between-meal nutrient boost. It provides protein and hydration without the heaviness of a full meal.
- **Homemade.** Save chicken carcasses and beef bones in a freezer bag until you have enough for a batch. Simmer in a pot with water, a splash of apple cider vinegar (to draw out minerals), and sea salt for 12-24 hours. Strain and store in jars.
