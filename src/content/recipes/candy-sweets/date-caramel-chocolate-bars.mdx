## Steps

1. Soak the pitted dates in warm water for 10 minutes to soften, then drain thoroughly. Add them to a food processor with the almond butter and sea salt. Process for 2-3 minutes, scraping down the sides, until you have a thick, sticky caramel paste. It should hold together when pressed but won't be perfectly smooth — small date pieces add texture.

2. Line a small baking dish or loaf pan with parchment paper. Press the date caramel into the pan in an even layer about half an inch thick. Press the chopped almonds firmly into the top of the caramel layer so they embed halfway in. Place the pan in the freezer for 15 minutes.

3. Melt the dark chocolate and coconut oil together using a double boiler or microwave in 20-second intervals, stirring until smooth.

4. Remove the caramel-almond slab from the freezer and lift it out using the parchment overhang. Cut into 10 rectangular bars with a sharp knife.

5. Using two forks, dip each bar into the melted chocolate, turning to coat all sides completely. Let excess chocolate drip off, then place the coated bar on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Sprinkle the tops with flaky sea salt before the chocolate sets.

6. Refrigerate the bars for at least 30 minutes until the chocolate is fully hardened. Store in a single layer or separated by parchment paper.

## Why It Works

Medjool dates are naturally caramel-flavored and packed with potassium, magnesium, and fiber. When blended with almond butter, they form a sticky, rich filling that mimics the texture of caramel nougat without any refined sugar or corn syrup. The dark chocolate shell snaps cleanly and delivers a hit of antioxidants, while the almonds add crunch, protein, and staying power that keeps you satisfied far longer than a conventional candy bar.

## Tips

- **Freeze before dipping.** The bars must be cold and firm before chocolate dipping or they'll fall apart on the forks. If they soften while you're working, pop them back in the freezer for 5 minutes.
- **Fork dipping technique.** Balance each bar on two forks and lower it into the chocolate. Tap the forks on the edge of the bowl to shake off excess. Slide the bar off onto parchment using a toothpick to nudge it.
- **Customize the crunch.** Swap almonds for peanuts, cashews, or toasted coconut flakes. Each gives the bars a different character while keeping the same caramel-chocolate structure.
