## Steps

1. Pour the coconut milk into a small saucepan and set over medium-low heat. Warm gently — do not let it boil.

2. Add the turmeric, grated ginger, cinnamon, and black pepper to the saucepan. Whisk continuously until the spices are fully dissolved and the milk turns a deep golden color.

3. Let the mixture simmer on low for 3-4 minutes so the spices bloom and the flavors meld together.

4. Remove from heat and pour through a fine mesh strainer into your favorite mug to catch any ginger fibers.

5. Stir in the raw honey once the milk has cooled slightly — keeping it below boiling preserves the honey's beneficial properties.

## Why It Works

Turmeric's active compound is fat-soluble, which is why pairing it with full-fat coconut milk dramatically increases absorption. The pinch of black pepper contains piperine, which further enhances bioavailability. Together, these ingredients create a warming, mineral-rich drink that supports the body's natural wind-down process at night.

## Tips

- **Always add pepper.** It may seem odd, but even a tiny pinch makes the turmeric far more effective — you won't taste it.
- **Add honey off the heat.** Stirring raw honey into liquid above 110°F degrades its delicate enzymes. Let the milk cool for a minute first.
- **Make a paste for speed.** Mix turmeric, cinnamon, ginger, and pepper with a splash of coconut oil into a paste. Store in a jar and scoop a teaspoon into warm milk for instant golden milk all week.
