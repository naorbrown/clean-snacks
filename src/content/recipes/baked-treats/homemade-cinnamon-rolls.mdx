## Steps

1. Combine the warm milk, honey, and yeast in a large bowl. Stir gently and let it sit for 5-10 minutes until the mixture becomes foamy, which confirms the yeast is active.

2. Add the melted butter (4 tablespoons), egg, sea salt, and flour to the yeast mixture. Stir until a shaggy dough forms, then turn it out onto a lightly floured surface and knead for 8-10 minutes until the dough is smooth, elastic, and slightly tacky. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover with a damp towel, and let it rise in a warm spot for 1 to 1.5 hours until doubled in size.

3. While the dough rises, prepare the filling by mixing the coconut sugar and cinnamon together in a small bowl. Once the dough has doubled, punch it down and roll it out on a floured surface into a large rectangle, roughly 16 by 12 inches. Spread the softened butter evenly over the surface, then sprinkle the cinnamon-sugar mixture over the entire rectangle, leaving a thin border along one long edge.

4. Starting from the long edge opposite the border, roll the dough tightly into a log. Use a sharp knife or unflavored dental floss to cut the log into 12 equal rolls. Place the rolls cut-side up in a buttered 9-by-13-inch baking dish, leaving a little space between each. Cover and let them rise for 30 minutes while you preheat the oven to 375°F.

5. Bake the rolls for 22-25 minutes until golden on top and cooked through. While they bake, make the frosting by beating together the cream cheese, softened butter, honey, and vanilla until smooth and fluffy. Spread the frosting generously over the warm rolls as soon as they come out of the oven, so it melts slightly into the crevices.

## Why It Works

This dough uses butter instead of industrial seed oils, giving the rolls a rich, tender crumb with clean saturated fat. Raw honey provides sweetness along with trace enzymes and minerals that refined white sugar lacks entirely. Coconut sugar in the filling delivers a deep caramel-like flavor with a lower glycemic index than white sugar, plus it retains small amounts of potassium, iron, and zinc. The cream cheese frosting stays simple — real cream cheese, butter, honey, and vanilla — with no powdered sugar, corn syrup, or artificial flavors. Whole milk in the dough contributes fat-soluble vitamins and creates a softer texture than skim milk would. Fine sea salt contains a broader mineral profile than standard table salt and balances the sweetness throughout.

## Tips

- **Make them overnight.** After cutting and placing the rolls in the pan, cover tightly with plastic wrap and refrigerate overnight. In the morning, pull them out, let them warm up for 30-45 minutes, and bake as directed for fresh hot rolls with minimal morning effort.
- **Freeze for later.** Baked rolls without frosting freeze beautifully for up to two months. Thaw at room temperature, warm in a 300°F oven for 10 minutes, then frost.
- **Swap the flour.** You can replace up to half the all-purpose flour with white whole wheat flour for more fiber without making the rolls dense or heavy.
