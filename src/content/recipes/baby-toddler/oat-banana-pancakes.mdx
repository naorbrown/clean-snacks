## Steps

1. In a bowl, mash the banana thoroughly with a fork until it forms a smooth paste with no large chunks remaining.

2. Add the egg to the mashed banana and whisk together until fully combined. The mixture should look like a smooth, pale yellow batter.

3. Add the oat flour and stir gently until just incorporated. Don't overmix — a few small lumps are fine and will keep the pancakes tender.

4. Heat the coconut oil in a non-stick pan over medium-low heat. The pan is ready when a drop of water sizzles gently (not aggressively).

5. Drop small spoonfuls of batter (about 1 tablespoon each) into the pan. Cook for 2-3 minutes until bubbles form on the surface and the edges look set, then flip and cook for another 1-2 minutes.

6. Let the pancakes cool to a safe temperature. Cut into strips or small pieces appropriate for your baby's age and skill level.

## Why It Works

These pancakes rely on banana for sweetness and moisture, egg for structure and protein, and oat flour for gentle fiber — no sugar, no milk, no added flavorings needed. The result is a soft, pillowy pancake that practically melts in the mouth, making it safe for babies who are still learning to chew. Oats are one of the gentlest grains for developing digestive systems.

## Tips

- **Make your own oat flour.** Pulse rolled oats in a blender for 30 seconds. It's cheaper, fresher, and you control the fineness. Finer flour makes softer pancakes.
- **Freeze for busy mornings.** Cool the pancakes completely, then freeze in a single layer on a baking sheet. Transfer to a freezer bag once solid. Reheat in a pan or toaster oven for 2 minutes.
- **Keep the heat low.** Baby pancakes burn easily because of the banana's natural sugars. Medium-low heat gives you an evenly golden pancake without any charred spots.
