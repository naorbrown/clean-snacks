## Steps

1. Peel the banana and place half of it in a small bowl. The riper the banana, the sweeter and softer the mash will be.

2. Halve the avocado, remove the pit, and scoop out a quarter of the flesh into the same bowl.

3. Mash both together with the back of a fork, pressing and stirring until you reach a smooth, creamy consistency with no large lumps.

4. For younger babies (6-8 months), press the mash through a fine mesh strainer for an even silkier texture. For older babies comfortable with soft textures, a fork mash is perfect.

5. Serve immediately at room temperature. This mash browns quickly, so prepare it right before feeding.

## Why It Works

Banana and avocado are two of the best first foods because they're naturally soft, nutrient-dense, and easy to digest. Avocado provides healthy fats essential for brain development, while banana offers natural sweetness that babies love along with potassium and gentle fiber. Together, they create a balanced, creamy mash that needs nothing else added.

## Tips

- **Serve fresh.** Avocado oxidizes and turns brown within 20-30 minutes. A squeeze of lemon slows this, but for babies under 12 months, it's best to just make it fresh each time.
- **Adjust the ratio.** If your baby prefers sweeter food, use more banana. For a richer, creamier mash, increase the avocado.
- **Texture progression.** Start silky smooth for new eaters, then gradually leave more texture as your baby develops chewing skills over the coming weeks.
