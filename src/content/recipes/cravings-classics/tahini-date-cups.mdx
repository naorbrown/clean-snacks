## Steps

1. Blend the pitted Medjool dates with the warm water and vanilla extract in a small food processor or blender until you get a thick, smooth paste. Scrape down the sides as needed. Set aside.

2. Melt the dark chocolate and coconut oil together in a double boiler or in the microwave in 20-second intervals, stirring between each until completely smooth.

3. Spoon about 1 teaspoon of melted chocolate into the bottom of each well of a 12-cup silicone mini muffin mold. Tilt and swirl to coat the bottom evenly. Freeze for 5 minutes until just set.

4. Drop a small spoonful (about 1/2 teaspoon) of the date paste into each cup, then add about 1/2 teaspoon of tahini on top. Press down gently with the back of a spoon to flatten — don't let the filling touch the sides of the mold.

5. Pour remaining melted chocolate over the filling in each cup, covering the tahini and date layers completely. Tap the mold against the counter to remove air bubbles and smooth the surface. Sprinkle flaky sea salt on top.

6. Refrigerate for at least 20 minutes until the chocolate is fully set. Pop the cups out of the mold and store in an airtight container in the refrigerator.

## Why It Works

Tahini is made from ground sesame seeds, delivering a rich source of calcium, iron, and healthy fats with a distinctive bitter-nutty flavor that pairs exceptionally with dark chocolate. Medjool dates act as a whole-food caramel — their high natural sugar content and sticky, fudgy texture create a gooey center without any refined sweeteners or corn syrup. The combination of sesame fat, date fiber, and dark chocolate polyphenols creates a candy cup that satisfies deeply while providing genuine nutritional density.

## Tips

- **Tahini consistency.** Stir your tahini jar thoroughly before measuring. The oil separation that happens in natural tahini is normal — you want the runny, well-mixed version for the best texture in these cups.
- **Date paste shortcut.** If your dates are very soft, you can skip the blender and just mash them with a fork. Add the warm water gradually.
- **Silicone molds are key.** Paper cupcake liners work but silicone molds give a much cleaner release and a more polished look.
- **Flavor twist.** Add 1/4 teaspoon of cinnamon to the date paste, or drizzle a thin line of tahini over the chocolate top before it sets for a visual accent.
