## Steps

1. In a large bowl, stir the warm water, yeast, and honey together. Let it sit for 5 minutes until foamy. Add the whole wheat flour, all-purpose flour, sea salt, and melted butter. Stir until a shaggy dough forms, then knead on a floured surface for 5 minutes until smooth and elastic. Cover with a damp towel and let rise in a warm spot for 20 minutes.

2. Preheat the oven to 425 degrees F and line two sheet pans with parchment paper. Bring a large pot of water to a boil and add the baking soda. Meanwhile, divide the dough into 8 equal pieces. Roll each piece into a rope about 12 inches long, then cut each rope into 5 bite-sized pieces.

3. Working in batches, drop the dough pieces into the boiling baking soda water for 20 to 30 seconds. Remove them with a slotted spoon and place them on the prepared pans. This step gives the pretzels their characteristic chewy crust and deep brown color.

4. Sprinkle the boiled pretzel bites with coarse sea salt. Bake for 12 to 14 minutes until they are deep golden brown on top. Brush with a thin layer of melted butter as soon as they come out of the oven.

5. While the pretzels bake, make the cheese dip. In a small saucepan over low heat, warm the milk until it just starts to steam. Add the shredded cheddar a handful at a time, stirring constantly until each addition is melted and smooth. Stir in the mustard powder and a pinch of sea salt. Keep warm over the lowest heat setting.

6. Serve the pretzel bites warm alongside the cheese dip.

## Why It Works

The baking soda bath is the secret to authentic pretzel texture. It creates an alkaline environment on the surface of the dough, which promotes the Maillard reaction during baking and produces that signature dark, glossy, chewy crust. Using a blend of whole wheat and unbleached flour gives the dough enough gluten structure to be chewy while adding a nutty depth that all-white-flour pretzels lack. The cheese dip is a simple stovetop preparation using real cheddar melted into whole milk, which delivers a smooth, creamy result without the sodium phosphate, canola oil, and food coloring found in jarred queso and stadium cheese sauces.

## Tips

- **Do not skip the baking soda bath.** It is the only step that makes these taste and look like real pretzels rather than plain bread rolls. The higher the concentration, the more pronounced the pretzel effect.
- **Dip consistency.** If the cheese dip thickens too much, stir in milk one tablespoon at a time until it reaches your desired consistency. Keep it on very low heat to prevent it from breaking.
- **Freezer friendly.** Freeze baked pretzel bites on a sheet pan, then transfer to a bag. Reheat from frozen at 375 degrees F for 8 minutes.
- **Everything seasoning.** Replace the coarse salt topping with everything bagel seasoning for a savory twist.
