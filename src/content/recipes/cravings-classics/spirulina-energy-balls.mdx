## Steps

1. Add the raw cashews to a food processor and pulse 8 to 10 times until broken into small crumbs but not powdered. You want some texture remaining, not cashew flour.

2. Add the pitted dates, spirulina powder, almond butter, melted coconut oil, lime juice, and sea salt to the food processor. Process for 30 to 45 seconds until the mixture forms a sticky, uniform dough that pulls away from the sides and clumps together.

3. Add the 1/4 cup of shredded coconut and pulse 3 to 4 times just to incorporate. Scrape down the sides as needed.

4. Scoop tablespoon-sized portions, squeeze firmly to compact, and roll into tight balls between your palms. Roll each ball in the remaining shredded coconut to coat the outside.

5. Place on a parchment-lined plate and refrigerate for at least 10 minutes to firm up. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator.

## Why It Works

Spirulina is a blue-green microalgae that packs an extraordinary nutritional punch — it's roughly 60% protein by weight and is one of the richest natural sources of phycocyanin, a pigment-protein with powerful antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Even a small dose delivers meaningful amounts of iron, B vitamins, and beta-carotene. Dates provide fast-digesting natural sugars for immediate energy alongside fiber to moderate the glycemic response. The combination of cashew fat, date sugar, and spirulina micronutrients makes these balls genuinely functional fuel rather than just another snack.

## Tips

- **Spirulina flavor.** Spirulina has a strong, oceanic taste. Start with 1 teaspoon if you're new to it and work up to the full amount. The lime juice helps mask the algae flavor significantly.
- **Color intensity.** These balls will be a vivid deep green. That's normal and entirely from the spirulina pigment — no dyes involved.
- **Date softness.** If your dates are dry or firm, soak them in warm water for 10 minutes and drain before processing. Soft dates blend much more easily and create a smoother dough.
- **Boost it.** Add 1 teaspoon of hemp seeds or a teaspoon of maca powder for additional nutritional depth without changing the flavor profile dramatically.
