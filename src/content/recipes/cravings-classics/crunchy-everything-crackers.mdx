## Steps

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper.

2. Combine the oats, sunflower seeds, sesame seeds, flax seeds, poppy seeds, dried garlic, dried onion, and sea salt in a bowl. Pour in the olive oil and water, then stir until everything is evenly hydrated. Let the mixture sit for 5 minutes so the flax seeds absorb the water and create a natural binding gel.

3. Spread the mixture onto the prepared baking sheet in a thin, even layer — aim for about 1/8-inch thickness. Use a second sheet of parchment on top and roll with a rolling pin to compress and flatten. Remove the top parchment and sprinkle with flaky salt.

4. Bake for 15-18 minutes until golden and dry throughout. The edges will brown first — that's normal. Remove from the oven and let cool completely on the pan. The crackers crisp up significantly as they cool.

5. Once fully cooled, break into irregular shards. Store in an airtight container at room temperature.

## Why It Works

Flax seeds mixed with water form a mucilaginous gel that acts as a natural binder, holding the cracker together without eggs or flour. Rolling the mixture paper-thin is what creates the shattering crunch — thick crackers stay chewy in the center. The combination of seeds and oats delivers fiber, healthy fats, and protein, which means these actually take the edge off hunger instead of leaving you reaching for more like empty-calorie snacks do. The everything bagel seasoning hits the same savory, aromatic notes your brain associates with satisfying food, making these genuinely effective for redirecting stress eating toward something that nourishes you.

## Tips

- **Roll as thin as possible.** The thinner the layer, the crunchier the cracker. If it's too thick in spots, those pieces will be chewy instead of crisp.
- **Watch the edges carefully.** The perimeter browns faster than the center. If the edges are getting too dark, you can break those pieces off and return the center to the oven for a few more minutes.
- **Customize the seasoning.** Swap the everything bagel mix for za'atar, ranch seasoning, or just rosemary and black pepper. The base recipe is a blank canvas.
- **Don't skip the resting step.** Those 5 minutes let the flax gel develop. Without it, the mixture won't hold together when baked.
