## Steps

1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F and line a sheet pan with parchment paper. In a large bowl, whisk together the whole wheat flour, cinnamon, baking soda, and sea salt.

2. In a small bowl, stir together the melted butter, honey, vanilla, and milk until combined. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and mix with a fork until a firm dough forms. If the dough is too crumbly, add milk one teaspoon at a time.

3. Place the dough between two sheets of parchment paper and roll it out to an even 1/8-inch thickness. Remove the top sheet and use a knife or pizza cutter to score the dough into rectangles, about 2 by 4 inches. Prick each rectangle several times with a fork to create the classic graham cracker pattern.

4. Transfer the parchment with the scored dough onto the sheet pan. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes until the edges turn deep golden brown and the crackers feel firm. Watch carefully since the honey can cause them to go from golden to burnt quickly.

5. Let the crackers cool completely on the pan. They will continue to crisp as they cool. Snap them apart along the scored lines once fully cooled.

## Why It Works

Original graham crackers were invented by Sylvester Graham in the 1800s as a simple whole wheat product, but modern commercial versions have drifted far from that origin, replacing whole grain with enriched flour and adding high-fructose corn syrup, soybean oil, and artificial flavors. This recipe returns to the original concept. Whole wheat flour delivers the nutty, slightly sweet flavor that graham crackers are known for, while raw honey provides enzymatic benefits that refined sweeteners cannot. The thin rolled dough and fork-pricked surface allow moisture to escape evenly during baking, producing a cracker that snaps cleanly and stays crisp for days.

## Tips

- **Uniform thickness is critical.** Uneven dough leads to some crackers burning while others stay soft. Use guide sticks or a marked rolling pin to maintain 1/8-inch thickness throughout.
- **Cinnamon sugar topping.** Before baking, brush the scored dough lightly with milk and sprinkle with a mix of cinnamon and coconut sugar for a sweeter version.
- **S'mores ready.** These are the perfect base for clean s'mores. Pair with dark chocolate and homemade marshmallows for a campfire treat.
- **Pie crust.** Pulse leftover crackers in a food processor with melted butter to make a graham cracker crust for cheesecake or pie.
