## Steps

1. If your dates are dry or firm, soak them in warm water for 10 minutes, then drain completely. Fresh, soft Medjool dates can skip this step — they should be sticky and pliable when squeezed.

2. Spread the walnuts in a food processor and pulse 8-10 times until they're coarsely ground — you want a mix of fine crumbs and small chunks for texture. Don't over-process into nut butter.

3. Add the pitted dates to the food processor with the walnuts. Process continuously for 30-60 seconds until the mixture starts to come together into a thick, sticky mass.

4. Add the cacao powder, vanilla extract, and sea salt. Process again until everything is evenly distributed and the dough holds together firmly when pressed between your fingers.

5. Line an 8x8 baking pan with parchment paper. Transfer the dough to the pan and press it into an even, compact layer using the back of a spoon or your hands. Press firmly — loose brownies crumble when cut.

6. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes to firm up, then lift out using the parchment paper and cut into 12 squares. Store in the fridge.

## Why It Works

Dates are naturally sticky and sweet, which means they bind the ingredients together and provide all the sweetness without any added sugar. When processed with walnuts, they create a dense, fudgy texture that genuinely mimics a brownie. The cacao powder delivers deep chocolate flavor, and the sea salt does what it always does — amplifies sweetness and adds complexity. These are energy-dense in the best way, packed with fiber, healthy fats, and minerals.

## Tips

- **Press hard.** The firmness of the final brownie depends entirely on how well you compact the dough into the pan. Loose pressing creates crumbly brownies. Really lean into it.
- **Pulse the walnuts first.** Adding dates to whole walnuts means the dates get over-processed while the walnuts are still in chunks. Processing the walnuts first ensures even texture.
- **Keep them cold.** These soften at room temperature and can get sticky. Store in the fridge and pull out 5 minutes before eating for the best fudgy texture.
