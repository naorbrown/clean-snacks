## Steps

1. Cut the chicken breast into even 1-inch cubes. Pat them completely dry with paper towels — surface moisture prevents browning and causes the oil to splatter.

2. Season the chicken cubes with sea salt and toss to distribute evenly.

3. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until it shimmers. Add the chicken cubes in a single layer — work in batches if needed. Don't move them for 3 minutes to develop a golden sear on the bottom.

4. Flip the pieces and cook for another 3-4 minutes until cooked through and golden on all sides. The internal temperature should reach 165°F (74°C).

5. While the chicken cooks, stir together the hot sauce and melted butter in a large bowl. The butter mellows the heat and creates that classic buffalo flavor — tangy, buttery, and just spicy enough.

6. Transfer the cooked chicken directly into the bowl with the buffalo sauce. Toss until every piece is evenly coated. Serve immediately alongside crisp celery sticks.

## Why It Works

Traditional buffalo sauce is simply hot sauce mixed with butter — two clean ingredients that create an iconic flavor. By pan-searing the chicken instead of deep frying, you get a golden exterior without the refined seed oils and breading. The butter in the sauce adds richness and tames the vinegar-forward heat of the hot sauce into something addictive. Celery sticks on the side aren't just garnish — their cool crunch provides genuine contrast to the warm, spicy chicken.

## Tips

- **Dry the chicken thoroughly.** Wet chicken steams instead of searing. Pat each piece dry with paper towels before seasoning. This is the difference between golden-brown bites and pale, rubbery ones.
- **Don't crowd the pan.** If the chicken pieces touch, they steam each other. Use a big skillet or cook in two batches. A good sear requires space and contact with the hot pan surface.
- **Adjust the heat ratio.** For milder bites, increase the butter and decrease the hot sauce. For serious heat, go the other direction. The sauce ratio is flexible — find what works for your heat tolerance.
