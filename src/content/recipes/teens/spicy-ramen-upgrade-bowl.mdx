## Steps

1. Bring a small pot of water to a boil for the egg. Gently lower the egg into the boiling water and cook for exactly 6 1/2 minutes for a jammy, soft-boiled yolk. Transfer immediately to a bowl of ice water and let it cool while you prepare everything else.

2. In a separate saucepan, heat the bone broth over medium heat. Add the minced garlic, coconut aminos, and chili paste. Stir to combine and bring to a gentle simmer — don't let it reach a full rolling boil, which can make the broth cloudy.

3. While the broth heats, cook the rice noodles according to package directions — most thin rice noodles only need 3-4 minutes in boiling water. Drain and rinse briefly under warm water to prevent sticking.

4. Place the cooked noodles in the bottom of a large bowl. Ladle the hot spicy broth over the noodles.

5. Peel the soft-boiled egg, cut it in half lengthwise, and place it on top of the noodles. Scatter the sliced green onions over the bowl and drizzle with the toasted sesame oil. Add more chili paste if you want it spicier.

## Why It Works

Instant ramen packets contain MSG, TBHQ (a petroleum-derived preservative), refined palm oil, and dehydrated flavoring with almost zero nutritional value. This upgraded bowl starts with bone broth, which is rich in collagen, glycine, and minerals like calcium and magnesium that support gut health and joint function. Rice noodles are naturally gluten-free and easier to digest than the fried wheat noodle blocks in instant ramen. The soft-boiled egg adds complete protein and choline, while coconut aminos provide the umami savoriness of soy sauce without the refined soy and wheat. Toasted sesame oil delivers flavor in small amounts without the inflammatory load of the seed oils used to fry instant noodle blocks.

## Tips

- **Nail the soft-boiled egg.** 6 1/2 minutes from the moment the egg hits boiling water gives you a set white and a perfectly jammy yolk. Even 30 seconds too long pushes it toward hard-boiled territory. Use a timer.
- **Multiply the recipe easily.** This scales linearly — just double or triple everything. Heat the broth in a larger pot and cook more noodles. Each person can customize their bowl with their preferred heat level.
- **Add more toppings.** Shredded leftover chicken, sliced mushrooms, baby spinach, or nori sheets all make this bowl more substantial. Prep extra toppings on Sunday and build bowls all week.
