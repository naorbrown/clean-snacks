## Steps

1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a large rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper.

2. Slice the sweet potatoes into even rounds about 1/4 inch thick. Toss them with olive oil and sea salt in a large bowl until every piece is coated.

3. Arrange the rounds in a single layer on the baking sheet, overlapping slightly like nacho chips. Bake for 20 minutes until the bottoms are golden and the edges begin to crisp.

4. Remove from the oven and scatter the black beans and shredded cheese evenly over the sweet potato rounds. Return to the oven for 5-7 minutes until the cheese is fully melted and bubbly.

5. Remove from the oven and immediately top with spoonfuls of salsa and sliced or mashed avocado. The cold toppings against the hot, cheesy base create a perfect contrast.

6. Serve straight from the sheet pan while everything is hot. Eat with your hands like regular nachos — grab a loaded round and go.

## Why It Works

Sweet potato rounds replace tortilla chips with something far more nutritious — they're packed with beta-carotene, fiber, and complex carbohydrates that provide lasting energy instead of a quick spike and crash. The cheese and beans add protein and fat, making this a legitimately balanced meal disguised as nachos. Baking the rounds first ensures they're crispy enough to hold the toppings without going soggy.

## Tips

- **Slice evenly.** Inconsistent thickness means some rounds burn while others stay soft. A mandoline makes this effortless, but a sharp knife and steady hand work fine.
- **Don't skip pre-baking.** If you add toppings to raw sweet potato rounds, they'll steam under the cheese and turn mushy. The 20-minute pre-bake is essential for structure and crunch.
- **Add cold toppings after.** Avocado and salsa go on after baking. If you bake them, the avocado turns bitter and the salsa loses its fresh flavor.
