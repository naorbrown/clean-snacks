## Steps

1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper. If you have two baking sheets, use both — overcrowding is the enemy of crispy fries.

2. Peel the sweet potatoes and cut them into uniform sticks about 1/4 inch thick and 3 inches long. Consistency in size is critical — uneven fries cook unevenly.

3. Pat the sweet potato sticks completely dry with a clean towel. Surface moisture creates steam in the oven, which prevents crisping. This step makes the biggest difference.

4. Toss the dried sticks with olive oil and sea salt in a large bowl until every piece is evenly coated.

5. Spread the fries in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet with space between each fry. They should not be touching — crowded fries steam instead of roast.

6. Bake for 15 minutes, then flip each fry and bake for another 10-12 minutes until the edges are golden brown and crispy. Serve immediately while hot.

## Why It Works

Sweet potatoes caramelize beautifully at high heat thanks to their natural sugar content. The key to crispy baked fries is eliminating moisture — drying them before oiling and giving them space on the sheet ensures the oven's dry heat crisps the exterior while the interior turns soft and creamy. Olive oil has a high enough smoke point for this temperature and adds a subtle richness, while sea salt brings out the potato's natural sweetness.

## Tips

- **Dry them thoroughly.** This is the number one tip for crispy fries. Wet sweet potatoes will steam and turn soggy no matter what else you do right.
- **Don't crowd the pan.** Give each fry breathing room. Use two baking sheets if needed. Touching fries trap steam between them and stay limp.
- **Cut evenly.** A fry twice as thick as its neighbor will be undercooked when the thin one is burnt. Take the time to cut consistently and everything finishes at the same time.
